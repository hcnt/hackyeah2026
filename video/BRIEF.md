## Intent

Internal pitch to align the team (HackYeah 2026, crypto track) on the product idea: an app
connecting event organizers and attendees. Attendees sign up with a crypto wallet, log in and
scan their face; a camera at the event runs face detection, an oracle feeds the smart contract,
and attendees get paid according to the organizer's rules (first 100 users, arrive between X
and Y, stay until X, ...). Organizers embed a wallet signup with their rules on their website.

Iterative: we go back and forth to agree on frames and interface before building.

User's frame outline (v1, verbatim intent):

1. Big hook — get people to come to the event.
2. Minimalistic, Geist font: "First 100 users get a coffee." Coffee → gift → T-shirt → gift bag,
   while 100 → 200 → 500 and so on.
3. Suddenly: "You choose the prize." (user wrote "You choose what price, bank" — read as prize)
4. A widget comes in: "Let us help you embed the rules on your website."
5. The widget says "First 100 people get 10 USDC", with a wallet signup button whose label is a
   carousel of MetaMask and the other popular wallets, switching.
"Let's finish there for now."

08 · How it works — still frames (v7.4 draft)

Flow only, no motion yet. No chain at the start: plain sentence → the sentence becomes a Contract square → it drops to the bottom and starts the chain → the oracle network arrives at the top → widgets stream in; each one links its address onto the chain and sends its photo up to the oracles. The widgets carry the real team faces (cut out, transparent); faces end up in the oracle network and never go on chain.
